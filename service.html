Our Services

Process

At Grace Construction Company, we begin by meeting with each client to make sure our approach, communication, and design goals align. Because the owner, architect, and contractor work closely together throughout the project, we believe it is essential to build a strong working relationship. Deacon Noah, the owner of Grace Construction, is a certified Liberian contractor. When we are brought into the design process early, we can help develop the project scope, timeline, and budget. During construction, we stay in close contact with the owner or architect to discuss progress, discoveries, and any changes to the work.

Construction

Full-Service Construction

We provide self-performed construction and construction-management services for residential, commercial, institutional, and multi-unit residential projects. Our work ranges from small remodels to custom builds, accessory dwelling units (ADUs), and longer-term estate development.

Planning

From Concept to Completion

We help with planning and budgeting, permits and approvals, inspections, negotiations, scheduling, and project management from the first concept through completion.

Remodels
